DroneVoice

A virtual Daisy Pod running the DroneVoice firmware: six detuned oscillators, a sub, stereo ladder filters, slow motion and a long reverb. Same DSP, same controls and same eight patch slots as the hardware.

Controls

Encoder turn
Change page. LED 1 shows the page colour.
Encoder press
Cycle waveform: saw, square, triangle, sine.
Button 1
Cycle the interval of the upper pair. On the Patch page, load the selected slot.
Button 2
Sub oscillator on or off. On the Patch page, hold for one second to save.
Knobs
Soft takeover: after a page change or a patch load, a knob does nothing until it passes the stored value, marked by the amber notch. LED 1 is dim until both knobs are picked up.
